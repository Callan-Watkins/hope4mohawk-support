STUDENT CLUB · APP SUPPORT

Here to help.

Hope 4 Mohawk is a small student club app for updates, gatherings, ways to serve, and Scripture. If something is wrong or you have a question, contact the club organizer.

Get in touch

For app problems, privacy requests, or a concern about a club post:

callans.creations1@gmail.com ↗

Please don’t include passwords, admin codes, or private student details in your message.

Common questions

How do I change my name?

In the app, open Settings → Name.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings → Your data → Delete my account and data. If that does not work, email us and describe the problem.

My admin code expired.

Admin codes can be used once and expire after ten minutes. Ask the club organizer for a new code. Do not send the code by email.

A post is inaccurate or concerning.

Email the link or post title and explain the issue. A club organizer can edit, unpin, or remove it.

For urgent help

This app and email are not emergency channels. For an immediate safety concern, use your school’s established reporting process or local emergency services.
