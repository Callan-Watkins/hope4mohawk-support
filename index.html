FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is it free?

Yes. There are no ads, purchases or subscriptions.

Is there an Android version?

The app is on iPhone and iPad today. An Android version is in the works.

How do I get club events in my calendar?

Open the Calendar tab and tap Add to Apple Calendar, then allow access. The app keeps its own "Hope 4 Mohawk" calendar up to date. You can turn it off in Settings any time.

How do I change my name?

In the app, open Settings → Name.

How do I turn notifications on or off?

Settings → Stay in the loop. You can also change them in the iPhone Settings app under Hope 4 Mohawk.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings → Your data → Delete my account and data. If that doesn't work, see the full steps or email us.

My admin code expired.

Admin codes can be used once and expire after ten minutes. Ask the club organizer for a new code. Don't send the code by email.

A post is wrong or concerning.

Email the post title and explain the issue. A club organizer can edit, unpin or remove it.
