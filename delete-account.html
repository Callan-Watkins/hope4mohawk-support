HOPE 4 MOHAWK · YOUR DATA

Delete your account.

Applies to the iPhone and Android apps

Hope 4 Mohawk does not need an email address or password. The app creates an anonymous account so it can load club posts. You can delete that account and its data at any time.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Hope 4 Mohawk and go to More, then Settings.
  2. Under Your data, tap Delete my account and data.
  3. Confirm. The app returns to the welcome screen.

This removes your anonymous account, the name and saved drafts on that device, and any admin profile, admin membership, code attempts, and posts linked to that account. If you were a club admin, the posts you published are deleted too. This cannot be undone. Used-code records may remain without an account identifier to help protect the code system.

If you can't use the app

If you uninstalled the app or can't open it, email callans.creations1@gmail.com with the subject "Hope 4 Mohawk deletion request". Because accounts are anonymous, we can only find an account if you can tell us the device and approximate date you first used the app, and, for an admin, your display name. Please do not include an admin code or sensitive student details.

What may remain

Service-provider logs and backups (for example connection and request logs kept by Supabase) may remain for their normal operational retention periods after deletion. Uninstalling the app does not by itself delete the server account; use the steps above first.
