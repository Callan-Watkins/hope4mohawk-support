HOPE 4 MOHAWK · PRIVACY POLICY

Your data, plainly.

Effective September 24, 2026

Hope 4 Mohawk is a small student club app. This policy explains what the app stores, why, and how to remove it. The club organizer can be reached at callans.creations1@gmail.com.

What the app uses

  • Student name: The name you enter is kept on your device to personalize the app. Students do not need to enter an email address or password.
  • Anonymous account: When the app loads club posts, Supabase creates an anonymous account. Its account identifier and session are used to access the club bulletin and protect admin-only actions.
  • Admin information: If you redeem an admin code, we store your display name, admin membership, and code-attempt records. Posts you publish store their content, time, and author name. Other app users can see published posts and author names.
  • On-device data: Unfinished admin drafts and the bundled Bible text stay on your device. The app does not create a server-side Bible reading history.

Why we use it

We use this information to show club posts, recognize club leaders, prevent unauthorized publishing, and help keep the service working. We do not use it to sell personal information or deliver targeted ads.

Service providers

Supabase provides authentication, database storage, and server functions for the app. It may process connection and operational data, such as IP addresses and request logs, to deliver and protect the service. This support website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process ordinary website access information. We do not place advertising or analytics scripts on this site.

Club posts may contain videos hosted by YouTube, Vimeo, or another HTTPS video source. A video player loads only after you choose to open it in the post. The video host may then receive connection and device information and may use cookies or similar technology under its own privacy policy. We do not download or rehost those videos.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep an active anonymous account and its related app data while you use the app. You can delete your account in Settings → Your data → Delete my account and data. That action removes the account, the preferred name and saved drafts on that device, and any admin profile, membership, code attempts, and posts linked to that account. Used-code records may remain without an account identifier to help protect the code system.

Service-provider logs and backups may remain for their normal operational retention periods after in-app deletion. If you cannot use the app’s deletion control or want help with a privacy request, email callans.creations1@gmail.com. Please do not include an admin code or sensitive student details in the request.

Club posts and concerns

Club leaders should not publish private care details or student conversations. If a post is inaccurate or raises a privacy or safety concern, contact the club organizer at the email above. A leader can edit or remove club posts.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page if the app or its data practices change. The effective date above will change when we do. For questions, use the support page.
